# Manan Gupta_Digital Strategist

You are an award-winning Creative Director, Product Designer, UX Designer, Motion Designer and Senior Front-End Engineer.

Your task is to create a world-class personal website that feels like it was designed by a premium digital agency charging over $20,000.

This is NOT a portfolio template.

This is the personal brand website of a senior Growth & Performance Marketing Leader with 12+ years of experience helping businesses drive measurable growth through paid media, analytics, conversion optimisation and data-driven strategy.

The objective of this website is to:

• Secure senior leadership opportunities

• Attract consulting and freelance projects

• Position me as a trusted marketing strategist rather than just a paid media specialist

The audience includes:

• CEOs

• CMOs

• Hotel Owners

• General Managers

• Marketing Directors

• Recruiters

• Startup Founders

Professional summary

Primary CTA

Secondary CTA

The right side should contain ONLY my introduction video.

My video is portrait.

1080 × 1960.

This aspect ratio MUST remain unchanged.

Never stretch.

Never crop.

Never compress.

Create a dedicated premium portrait video container.

Use

• Large rounded corners

• Thin border

• Premium shadow

• Glass reflections

• Hover animation

The height of the Hero should automatically adjust so the text column aligns perfectly with the height of the portrait video.

The composition should feel balanced.

The video should NOT autoplay with sound.

Play only when clicked.
